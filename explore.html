Values are from the simulation's last heartbeat (lattice Boltzmann). “Healthy” is the healthy artery at the same blood pressure. Blood pressure is the mean pressure entering the artery: normal 95 mmHg (calibrated so the healthy artery draws the sourced 240 mL/min); stroke 118 and severe 146 mmHg are the average and highest of 7 emergency stroke patients on arrival (Sen et al., West J Emerg Med 2014). Brain tissue blood flow is the MCA flow per 100 g of the brain it feeds (about 175 g, sized so the healthy artery at normal pressure gives the normal 70.8); its state uses CT-perfusion values (Ionita et al., Endovascular Today 2009): at least 64.8 normal, 24.6–64.8 penumbra, below 24.6 infarct core. The model has no collateral vessels, so a blocked artery's tissue gets no flow at all here. The 90 % clot's 0.2 mm throat is only ~4 grid cells across: its numbers are approximate.
